FAQ

Questions, answered.

What can I upload?

Any static interface as PNG, JPG, JPEG or WEBP: website screenshots, mobile app screens, dashboards, wireframes or design mockups. Lumen detects the device type automatically from the aspect ratio.

What standards does the analysis use?

Accessibility checks follow WCAG 2.1 contrast and sizing guidance; usability scoring is mapped to Nielsen's 10 heuristics; hierarchy analysis evaluates CTA prominence, grouping, alignment and whitespace.

Can I export the results?

Yes, generate a polished PDF or CSV report, and export the extracted design system as JSON design tokens ready to drop into your codebase.

Is my design data private?

Uploads are processed for analysis only and never used to train models. Team workspaces support role based access control.
